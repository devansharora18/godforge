# Theme — Squeeze

**Material:** forest-green paper with real chroma; warm white ink; Gabarito 800 heads and figures over Manrope; soft rounded tiles a step lighter or deeper; fruit photographed bright and close; juicy peach controls.
**Not when:** the page must look neutral, or the content is regulatory.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(38% 0.090 154)     paper-2   oklch(44% 0.086 154)   paper-3  oklch(32% 0.080 154)
ink        oklch(97% 0.010 90)      muted     oklch(82% 0.030 120)
accent     peach 56 · chroma 0.15              accent-ink  oklch(26% 0.05 56)
edges      radius 18px tiles, 999px controls · depth none · rules none
```

## Type

- Display: Gabarito 800 at 40–84px, leading 1.0, sentence case, −0.01em.
- Body: Manrope 400 at 17px / 1.65, 52–64ch. Warm white, never pure white.
- Figures: Gabarito 800 at 56–84px when the number is the content.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Tiles are the paper one step lighter or deeper; never white, never a shadow.
- Radius is generous: 18px tiles, fully round controls. Nothing sharp on the page.
- Photographs are bright, close, sliced fruit — full colour against the green.
- No rules at all; separation is lightness only.

## Range

- A flavour or plan row where each is a tile in a different step of green.
- A number at 84px in warm white with one line of copy under it.
- A photograph bleeding off the tile edge, fruit cropped past the frame.

## Motion

Soft and quick: 220ms on tile lightness and 180ms on the control scale to 0.98.

## Voice

Fresh, direct, sensory but concrete. Name the flavour and the quantity.

## Signature

Depth by lightness on one hue: every surface is the same green, stepped lighter or deeper, so the page never shows a white card.

## Avoid

White surfaces, shadows, rules, sharp corners, a second hue, muted photography, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Horizontal band sequence · Full-bleed band
