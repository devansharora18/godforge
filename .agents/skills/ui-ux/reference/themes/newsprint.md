# Theme — Newsprint

**Material:** pale newsprint stock; warm black ink; Zilla Slab heads over a Spectral body with Libre Franklin agate for listed figures; justified columns under hairline column rules; a double rule; one dried-brick signal.
**Not when:** the page is a product tour, or the content is shorter than a page.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(92% 0.014 85)      paper-2   oklch(88% 0.016 85)
ink        oklch(22% 0.010 60)      muted     oklch(48% 0.014 60)
accent     brick 32 · chroma 0.13              accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.008 32)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px column rules, one 3px double rule
```

## Type

- Display: Zilla Slab 600 at 32–64px, leading 1.08, sentence case.
- Body: Spectral 400 at 16.5px / 1.55, justified, 40–48ch per column.
- Agate: Libre Franklin 400 at 13px, tabular, for stock tables and listings.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Column rules at 1px between columns, full height; a 3px double rule above the fold.
- Photographs are halftone-ish grey, cropped square, sitting inside a column, never bleeding.
- No fills, no cards, no shadows: the columns are the structure.
- Paper grain at 4%.

## Range

- A listings column: three lines per entry, agate figures on the right.
- A pull quote across two columns, slab head above, attribution below the rule.
- A stock or score table, four columns, ruled on both sides.

## Motion

None. Links underline on hover in 120ms; that is all.

## Voice

Reported, plain, dense. The head carries the fact; the lede carries the who and where.

## Signature

The justified column with a hairline rule beside it, set in Spectral, so the page reads as a printed sheet rather than a web page.

## Avoid

Centred hero, cards, pills, gradients, colour photography, sans body copy, tracked labels.

## Pairs with

Editorial columns · Ledger / index · Colophon close
