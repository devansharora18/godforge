# Theme — Midnight

**Material:** midnight-blue ground to every edge; cool white ink; Mona Sans expanded at 300 over its normal width; square-cut surfaces in lightness steps; photographs as moonlight; one azure signal.
**Not when:** the page must feel bright, playful, or printed.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(18% 0.040 264)     paper-2   oklch(23% 0.044 264)
ink        oklch(97% 0.004 250)     muted     oklch(74% 0.014 255)
accent     azure 230 · chroma 0.14              accent-ink  oklch(18% 0.03 230)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules none, lightness steps
```

## Type

- Display: Mona Sans Expanded 300 at 40–80px, leading 1.06, sentence case, −0.01em.
- Body: Mona Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 54–66ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- The display is a width and a light weight, not a second family.

## Surfaces

- Surfaces are the ground stepped lighter; three steps, no borders, no radius.
- Photographs are moonlight-graded: blue, low contrast, dark, no radius.
- No rules and no shadows; the edge between two surfaces is a lightness change only.
- The azure appears once per screen, as a small mark or a single word set in it.

## Range

- A three-column comparison where each column is a lightness step.
- A statement at 72px in expanded light, alone on the ground.
- A list of capabilities in rows that step lighter on hover.

## Motion

Slow: 320ms on lightness change. Nothing moves position.

## Voice

Calm, exact, expansive. Say what the system does, at what scale.

## Signature

Depth without shadow: every surface is the same blue at a different lightness, so the page reads as one material cut at three depths.

## Avoid

Glow, neon, pure black, white cards, borders, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Full-bleed band · Console / table
