# Theme — Coral

**Material:** warm grey paper on a felt desk; cotton stationery lit from the left; General Sans throughout with Geist Mono figures; letterpress card controls; coral pressed into the stock for the one settled state.
**Not when:** the page is a game, a gallery, or needs a dark mode first.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(90% 0.010 55)      paper-2   oklch(95% 0.008 60)
ink        oklch(27% 0.014 50)      muted     oklch(55% 0.012 55)
accent     coral 30 · chroma 0.13               accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.010 30)
edges      radius 3px cards, 999px pills · depth 1px press · rules 1px at 12% ink
```

## Type

- Display: General Sans 600 at 36–68px, leading 1.06, sentence case, −0.015em.
- Body: General Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.65, 54–64ch.
- Figures: Geist Mono 400 at 14px, tabular, for money and counts.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Stationery: white cards with a 1px hairline and a 1px letterpress edge, sitting on the warm grey.
- Coral is pressed into the stock: an inset shadow of 1px, never a glow or a fill on large areas.
- Photographs are lit from the left with a soft even falloff, placed on the desk.
- No radius above 3px except pills; no gradient anywhere.

## Range

- An invoice or ledger: line items in mono, total in the display face.
- A single receipt or card photographed on the felt, cropped square.
- A three-step setup where each step is a numbered letterpress card.

## Motion

Quiet: 200ms on card press and 240ms opacity on load. Nothing slides.

## Voice

Warm, plain, businesslike. Say the amount, the date, the terms.

## Signature

The letterpress edge: every control and card carries a 1px pressed edge, as if the coral were struck into cotton stock.

## Avoid

Dark grounds, gradients, heavy shadows, saturated fills on large areas, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Diptych · Console / table
