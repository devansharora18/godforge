# Theme — Wayfind

**Material:** cool grey paper; blue-black ink; Chivo Mono throughout; square 2px ink borders; ink plates; cobalt for what is live and amber for figures printed on ink.
**Not when:** the page is expressive, editorial, or needs a serif voice.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(91% 0.006 250)     paper-2   oklch(86% 0.008 250)
ink        oklch(22% 0.020 264)     muted     oklch(50% 0.014 264)
accent     cobalt 264 · chroma 0.18    on-ink  amber 80 · chroma 0.14
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 2px ink, square joins
```

## Type

- Display: Chivo Mono 600 at 28–56px, leading 1.1, sentence case, 0.01em.
- Body: Chivo Mono 400 at 15px / 1.7, 62–74ch. Mono is the material here, so mono labels are legal.
- Figures: Chivo Mono 500, tabular, on ink plates in amber.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Ink plates: 2px bordered boxes filled with ink, holding figures in amber.
- Everything is a right angle; borders are 2px and meet at the corner.
- Photographs appear once, cropped square, inside an ink frame.
- No shadow, no radius, no gradient; the plate is the depth.

## Range

- A directory: name, category, floor — mono rows, 2px rules.
- A single figure on an ink plate at 56px in amber.
- A plan or map drawn in 1px lines inside a 2px frame.

## Motion

None. Hover inverts a plate in 100ms.

## Voice

Literal, navigational, exact. Say the floor, the direction, the hours.

## Signature

The ink plate with amber figures: numbers printed on solid ink, the only filled surface and the only second colour.

## Avoid

Serif, rounded corners, shadows, gradients, colour photography, a third accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Console / table · Asymmetric split · Horizontal band sequence
