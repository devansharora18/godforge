# Theme — Vellum

**Material:** parchment paper; a long tan fibre wash across bands; umber ink; Grenze Gotisch heads over Hanken Grotesk, with Grenze roman for statements; hairline and double rules; one ultramarine pigment.
**Not when:** the page is a utility, a dashboard, or anything read in a hurry.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(90% 0.028 82)      paper-2   oklch(85% 0.034 78)
ink        oklch(28% 0.030 62)      muted     oklch(54% 0.026 62)
accent     ultramarine 268 · chroma 0.14       accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.008 268)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px hairline, one 4px double rule
```

## Type

- Display: Grenze Gotisch 600 at 34–64px, leading 1.06, sentence case. A short head only — blackletter does not wrap well.
- Statement: Grenze 400 at 22–28px for a sentence that would otherwise be a paragraph.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 400 at 16.5px / 1.68, 52–62ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Bands carry a tan fibre wash at 8% with an irregular edge; the paper stays visible.
- A 4px double rule marks the opening section; hairlines everywhere else.
- Illustrations or photographs are single-colour prints in umber, never full colour.
- No shadows; depth comes from the wash and the double rule.

## Range

- A recipe or record: ingredient left, quantity right, double rule above.
- A quote set in blackletter at 40px, two lines maximum.
- A timeline drawn as a vertical hairline with umber marks.

## Motion

None. The page is a document; links underline in 120ms.

## Voice

Old, plain, precise. Dates in full, quantities in figures, no modern jargon.

## Signature

The double rule and the fibre wash together: a section opens like a page of a bound record book.

## Avoid

Full-colour photographs, gradients, rounded corners, shadows, a second pigment, long blackletter heads, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Editorial columns · Ledger / index · Full-bleed band
