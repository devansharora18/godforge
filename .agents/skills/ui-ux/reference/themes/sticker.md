# Theme — Sticker

**Material:** warm off-white paper; warm black ink; Bowlby One SC over Sofia Sans; white die-cut borders on hard offset shadows; hot pink, lime and ink fills set with type.
**Not when:** the page is a checkout, a contract, or anything a lawyer reads.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(95% 0.018 85)      paper-2   oklch(90% 0.024 85)
ink        oklch(21% 0.020 50)      muted     oklch(50% 0.020 50)
accent     pink 2 · chroma 0.20     second  lime 125 · chroma 0.19
edges      radius 20px die cuts · depth hard 5px offset · rules none
```

## Type

- Display: Bowlby One SC 400 at 32–72px, leading 1.0, capitals by design. Short heads only.
- Body: Sofia Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.6, 50–62ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Never set Bowlby One SC for anything longer than four words.

## Surfaces

- Die cut: every panel has a 4px white border and 20px radius, with 5px of solid ink offset behind.
- Fills are flat pink, flat lime, or ink; one fill per panel.
- Photographs are cut out with a die edge, never rectangular.
- Paper is warm off-white; nothing is pure white except the die border.

## Range

- A three-up row of stickers, each with one fact in the display face.
- A price as a single sticker rotated 3° on the paper.
- A testimonial on an ink panel with a lime mark, white die edge around it.

## Motion

One nudge: hover pushes a sticker 2px into its shadow in 120ms. Nothing bounces or spins.

## Voice

Blunt, warm, funny in one line. Say the thing, then stop.

## Signature

The die cut: everything on the page looks peeled and stuck down, with a white edge and a solid shadow.

## Avoid

Soft shadows, gradients, rectangular photos, a fourth colour, long display heads, subtle anything.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Diptych · Horizontal band sequence
