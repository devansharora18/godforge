# Theme — Terrazzo

**Material:** off-white paper; white card surfaces ruled in ink over a hard offset; wide Syne heads over Onest; JetBrains Mono for codes and millimetres; surfaces photographed flat-on in true colour; rust is the one signal.
**Not when:** the page is dark-mode first, or the content is emotional.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(92% 0.008 82)      paper-2   oklch(98% 0.004 82)
ink        oklch(22% 0.010 60)      muted     oklch(50% 0.012 60)
accent     rust 38 · chroma 0.14                accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.010 38)
edges      radius 2px · depth 3px hard offset · rules 1px ink over the offset
```

## Type

- Display: Syne 700 wide at 34–68px, leading 1.02, sentence case, −0.02em.
- Body: Onest 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 54–66ch.
- Codes and dimensions: JetBrains Mono 400 at 13px, tabular.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- White cards sit on the off-white paper, ruled 1px, each with a 3px hard offset in ink.
- Surfaces are photographed flat-on, true colour, cropped to the slab edge.
- Radius is 2px — enough to read as a product, not as a card.
- No shadows, no gradients; the offset is the depth.

## Range

- A swatch row: material name, finish, size — mono figures in a ruled table.
- A flat-on photograph of a slab at full width with three rust marks.
- A price in Syne at 56px beside a ruled specification.

## Motion

Firm: 160ms on card offset when pressed. Nothing lifts.

## Voice

Material, plain, exact. Say the finish, the size, the lead time.

## Signature

The hard offset with a 1px rule: white surfaces float 3px above the paper on a slab of ink, never on a soft shadow.

## Avoid

Soft shadows, gradients, rounded cards, a second accent, lifestyle photography, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Console / table · Horizontal band sequence
