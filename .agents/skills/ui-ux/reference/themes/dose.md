# Theme — Dose

**Material:** warm morning paper with a fibre grain and window light; translucent tinted plastic; green-black ink; Schibsted Grotesk over Instrument Sans with DM Mono pharmacy labels; moulded controls; one pharmacy green.
**Not when:** the page is entertainment, or the product is not about routine or care.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(93% 0.016 88)      paper-2   oklch(97% 0.010 88)
ink        oklch(26% 0.030 160)     muted     oklch(54% 0.024 160)
accent     pharmacy 160 · chroma 0.12          accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.008 160)
edges      radius 16px moulded · depth 2px soft · rules 1px at 10% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Schibsted Grotesk 600 at 34–64px, leading 1.06, sentence case.
- Body: Instrument Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.68, 54–64ch.
- Labels: DM Mono 400 at 12px — the one exception, because a pharmacy label is the material. Sentence case.
- Numbers in tables: DM Mono 400, tabular.

## Surfaces

- Translucent tinted plastic: 16% green over the paper, 16px radius, 2px soft edge.
- One card per page carries the fibre grain and the window light.
- Controls are moulded: 2px inset edge, no drop shadow, they press flat.
- Photographs are bright, soft, shot on the paper.

## Range

- A schedule: time, dose, name — mono figures, moulded rows.
- A single card as the object of the page, photographed at 45°.
- A three-step routine where each step is a translucent panel.

## Motion

Warm and gentle: 260ms opacity, 180ms press. No movement of layout.

## Voice

Calm, plain, exact. Say the dose, the time, the instruction — never "wellness".

## Signature

Translucent tinted panels: surfaces you can see the paper through, so the page feels like a card in sunlight.

## Avoid

Saturated colour, dark grounds, sharp corners, gradient fills, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Asymmetric split · Editorial columns
