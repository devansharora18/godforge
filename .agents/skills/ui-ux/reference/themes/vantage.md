# Theme — Vantage

**Material:** blue-black ground; a live point cloud of the real thing as the hero's light, lit by a sweep that passes over it; pale steel ink; Geologica with sharpened corners at a light weight; hairline frames with corner ticks; one lime signal for what was found.
**Not when:** the page is consumer, printed, or needs warmth.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(19% 0.030 235)     paper-2   oklch(24% 0.032 235)
ink        oklch(93% 0.010 240)     muted     oklch(70% 0.018 240)
accent     lime 126 · chroma 0.17               accent-ink  oklch(19% 0.04 126)
edges      radius 2px · depth none · rules 1px hairline at 14% ink, corner ticks
```

## Type

- Display: Geologica 400 sharpened corners at 36–72px, leading 1.06, sentence case.
- Body: Geologica 400 at 16.5px / 1.6, 54–64ch, steel white at 90%.
- Measurements: Geist Mono 400 at 13px for scan figures and coordinates.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Hairline frames at 14% with 6px corner ticks — the measurement language of the page.
- The point cloud is the only light source; no glows, no gradients, no radial halos.
- Panels are 2px radius; nothing is filled except the lime mark.
- Photographs are replaced by captures of the real thing, not stock imagery.

## Range

- A scan table: point, distance, confidence — mono figures.
- A capture at full width with a corner-ticked frame and three lime markers.
- A three-step capture-to-model sequence, each step framed.

## Motion

The sweep: a 4s light pass over the cloud, paused under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Panels fade at 240ms.

## Voice

Technical, factual, measured. Say the resolution, the area, the error.

## Signature

The lit point cloud: a real scan used as the hero's only light, so the page's brightness comes from the data.

## Avoid

Glow, radial halos, gradients, stock photography, rounded cards, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Console / table · Asymmetric split
