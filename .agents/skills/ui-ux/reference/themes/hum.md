# Theme — Hum

**Material:** grained cream pulled toward pear; torn paper edges with a pale fibre core; card-stock controls that press down; blue-black ink; Chillax throughout; three accents that never blend; a mint for growth.
**Not when:** the page carries a serious transaction, or the audience is skeptical of friendly software.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(95% 0.040 95)      paper-2   oklch(90% 0.050 95)
ink        oklch(25% 0.030 265)     muted     oklch(55% 0.025 265)
accent     yellow 95 · chroma 0.17  second  pink 0 · chroma 0.14  third  mint 160 · chroma 0.12
edges      radius 14px tiles · depth 2px press · rules none
```

## Type

- Display: Chillax 700 at 36–72px, leading 1.05, sentence case, −0.01em.
- Body: Chillax 400 at 17px / 1.6, 54–66ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- One family throughout; hierarchy comes from size and colour, never from a second face.

## Surfaces

- Torn paper: 8px irregular edges on tiles, with a 1px pale fibre line inside the tear.
- Controls are card stock: 14px radius, 2px offset shadow, and they press 2px on active.
- Accents are separate fields — a yellow tile, a pink tile, a mint mark — never mixed on one surface.
- Photographs are warm, bright, cropped close, with a torn edge on one side.

## Range

- A set of three steps, each a torn tile, each a different accent.
- A testimonial in a bubble with a torn edge and a real name.
- A price in Chillax at 64px with the mint mark on the available tier.

## Motion

Playful but contained: 180ms press, 200ms tile lift of 2px. Nothing bounces, nothing loops.

## Voice

Friendly and plain. Second person, short sentences, no exclamation marks.

## Signature

The torn edge with its fibre core: every surface ends in a paper tear, so the page reads as cut card rather than rendered boxes.

## Avoid

Flat corners, gradient fills, grey shadows, neutral only, a fourth accent, emoji, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Horizontal band sequence · Asymmetric split
