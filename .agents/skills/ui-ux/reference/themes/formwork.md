# Theme — Formwork

**Material:** warm grey concrete paper with a speck grain; near-black ink; Oswald capitals over Archivo; square cast edges and hard shadows; hard black-and-white photographs of poured concrete; one safety orange.
**Not when:** the page is soft, personal, or about a service.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(80% 0.008 80)      paper-2   oklch(86% 0.006 80)
ink        oklch(20% 0.006 70)      muted     oklch(48% 0.006 70)
accent     orange 38 · chroma 0.19             accent-ink  oklch(17% 0.02 38)
edges      radius 0 · depth 3px hard at 0 blur · rules 2px ink
```

## Type

- Display: Oswald 600, capitals, leading 0.94, 36–72px, 0.01em.
- Body: Archivo 400 at 16px / 1.55, 52–64ch. Never condensed body copy.
- Figures: Archivo at 500, tabular, set against the ruled grid.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Speck grain at 6% over the ground; the texture is the material, not a gradient.
- Every panel has a cast edge: 1px darker top, 3px hard shadow at zero blur.
- Photographs are black and white, cropped to the panel, never matted.
- Rules are 2px and clip their content tight — nothing floats.

## Range

- A specification table: dimension, mix, cure — one row per line, ruled.
- A drawing or plan as a full-width black-and-white plate.
- A figure set at display size against a ruled measure, like a site note.

## Motion

None on entry. A panel lifts by 2px on hover in 120ms, shadow unchanged.

## Voice

Measured, technical, exact. Millimetres and dates, never "robust" or "built to last".

## Signature

The cast edge: every panel sits on 3px of unblurred shadow with a darker lip, as if struck from a mould.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, colour photography, thin type, blurred shadows, a second orange mark, tracked capitals in labels.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Console / table · Horizontal band sequence
