# Theme — Almanac

**Material:** cool slate stock with a pale printed sheet; blue-black ink; Hanken Grotesk heads over Newsreader prose, mono for figures; two rule weights, square corners, one chart magenta.
**Not when:** the page is a launch, a demo, or anything that has to feel warm.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(84% 0.015 245)     paper-2   oklch(97% 0.006 250)
ink        oklch(24% 0.030 255)     muted     oklch(52% 0.020 250)
accent     magenta 350 · chroma 0.20            accent-ink  oklch(98% 0.005 350)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px hairline, 5px ink
```

## Type

- Display: Hanken Grotesk 700, sentence case, −0.02em. Never capitals.
- Body: Newsreader 400 at 17px / 1.62, 60–70ch.
- Figures: mono only inside tables; never mono for labels.
- Labels: the body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Square corners; the only inset is a 2px step on the printed sheet.
- Two rules do the structure: a 1px hairline and the 5px ink rule that opens a section.
- Photographs are muted, matted on the sheet with an even 24px mount, never full bleed.
- No shadows. A sheet edge is a hairline, not a shadow.

## Range

- A table of real figures with rules drawn between rows.
- A specimen line: term at 40px, definition at body size beside it.
- A keyed index with two-digit folios and dotted leaders.

## Motion

One 240ms fade with an 8px rise per section head. No stagger past three rows.

## Voice

Plain, dated, sourced. A figure carries its year and its source or it is absent.

## Signature

The pale printed sheet: a light block inset on the slate stock that holds the page's one real table.

## Avoid

Cards, pill controls, rounded photographs, drop shadows, an accent outside the magenta, tracked capitals anywhere.

## Pairs with

Ledger / index · Console / table · Editorial columns
