# Theme — Arcade

**Material:** aubergine CRT ground; lilac-white ink; Bungee capitals printing a split RGB edge over Chivo; Silkscreen for figures; hard offset shadows; ember and acid signals.
**Not when:** the page carries money, health, or a legal commitment.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(21% 0.070 312)     paper-2   oklch(27% 0.070 312)
ink        oklch(93% 0.020 300)     muted     oklch(74% 0.030 305)
accent     ember 40 · chroma 0.19   second  acid 118 · chroma 0.18
edges      radius 0 · depth hard 6px offset · rules 2px ink
```

## Type

- Display: Bungee 400, capitals, leading 0.92, 40–96px. Chivo 700 for a second line.
- Body: Chivo 400 at 16.5px / 1.55, 52–64ch. Never more than three lines together.
- Figures: Silkscreen 400, tabular, at sizes where it stays legible — scores, lives, credits.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- The CRT wash: a 12% scanline gradient over the ground, never over text.
- Every panel casts 6px of solid ink at 45°, no blur.
- Accents appear as whole blocks and as a 1px split edge on the display only.
- Photographs are tinted to the accent and reduced to 4 colours.

## Range

- A score sequence as a list of ruled rows, Silkscreen figures on the right.
- A cabinet mock built from real content — the machine's own screen, not a drawing.
- A price lineup with each tier on a different accent block.

## Motion

Hover steps the offset shadow 2px in 90ms, like a button press. No glitter, no bounce, no parallax.

## Voice

Playful and direct, in the product's own nouns. Say the game, the round, the score.

## Signature

The split RGB edge: the display's capitals carry a 1px magenta and cyan offset, as if the CRT is a hair out of alignment.

## Avoid

Bounce easing, sparkles, a third signal, rounded corners, soft shadows, emoji as icons, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Horizontal band sequence · Console / table
