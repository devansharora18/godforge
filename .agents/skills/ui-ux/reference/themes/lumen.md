# Theme — Lumen

**Material:** violet-black ground; near-white ink; lowercase Instrument Serif heads a step large over Geist and Geist Mono; hairline wells; a faint blueprint grid; one molten brass signal and one rose verb.
**Not when:** the page must feel warm, everyday, or local.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(17% 0.030 290)     paper-2   oklch(22% 0.034 290)
ink        oklch(96% 0.006 290)     muted     oklch(72% 0.014 290)
accent     brass 62 · chroma 0.11    verb   rose 350 · chroma 0.14
edges      radius 6px wells · depth none · rules 1px at 12% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif 400, lowercase, leading 1.1, 44–92px. The lowercase head is the voice.
- Body: Geist 400 at 16px / 1.62, 54–64ch.
- Measurements: Geist Mono 400 at 13px, tabular, for anything quantified.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked — mono only for figures.

## Surfaces

- A blueprint grid at 32px and 3% across the ground, visible only where nothing sits on it.
- Wells are hairline outlines at 12% ink with 6px radius; nothing is filled.
- Photographs are dim, violet-graded, no radius, sitting flush in a well.
- No shadows and no glow; the light comes from the brass marks.

## Range

- A capability list where each row is a hairline well and a mono figure.
- A single sentence in the display serif at 80px, lowercase, ending in the rose verb.
- A diagram of the system in mono, drawn on the grid.

## Motion

Restrained: 240ms opacity on wells, 120ms colour on links. Nothing scales.

## Voice

Considered, technical, lowercase in spirit. Precise claims, no promises.

## Signature

The rose verb: one verb in one link colour, appearing once, marking the only action the page wants.

## Avoid

Uppercase heads, glow, gradients, white surfaces, a third colour, mono for labels, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Oversized type · Console / table · Asymmetric split
