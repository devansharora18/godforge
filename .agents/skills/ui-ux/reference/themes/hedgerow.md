# Theme — Hedgerow

**Material:** deep leaf-green paper; cream ink; Prata heads over Manrope; low-key photographs in true colour; square flat surfaces with pill controls; one chartreuse signal deep enough to carry cream text.
**Not when:** the page must work on paper-white, or the brand is already green.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(26% 0.040 158)     paper-2   oklch(31% 0.036 158)
ink        oklch(94% 0.014 100)     muted     oklch(76% 0.020 130)
accent     chartreuse 118 · chroma 0.14 · L 66   accent-ink  oklch(20% 0.03 118)
edges      radius 6px on surfaces, 999px on controls · depth 1px top highlight
```

## Type

- Display: Prata 400 at 38–72px, leading 1.04, sentence case. High contrast against the ground is the whole voice.
- Body: Manrope 400 at 16.5px / 1.65, 54–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Never cream at full strength for small text; muted sits two steps below ink.

## Surfaces

- Surfaces are the paper a step lighter, never white and never a shadow.
- Square flat panels with 6px radius; controls are the only pills on the page.
- Photographs are low-key, true colour, and darker than the ground so they sink in.
- One 1px highlight along a panel's top edge, nothing else.

## Range

- A list of named varieties, each with a one-line note, on the darker panel.
- A single photograph at full width with the caption in the margin, cream on green.
- A quote set in Prata at 56px against the paper, no quote marks.

## Motion

Slow and few: 400ms opacity on images and panels, nothing on text.

## Voice

Grounded, observational, unhurried. Name the plant, the field, the month.

## Signature

The inverted surface: cards are lifted from the paper by lightness, so the page never shows a white rectangle.

## Avoid

White surfaces, glows, drop shadows, more than one chartreuse mark, cream text under 4.5:1, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Diptych · Editorial columns
