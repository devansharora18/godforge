# Theme — Blueprint

**Material:** prussian-blue sheet; white line ink; Chakra Petch capitals over IBM Plex Sans with mono measurements; square double keylines and dimension strings; photographs printed as cyanotypes; the white line is the only signal.
**Not when:** the page is consumer, playful, or needs warm colour.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(30% 0.090 256)     paper-2   oklch(35% 0.085 256)
ink        oklch(97% 0.006 250)     muted     oklch(84% 0.030 250)
accent     none — the ink is the signal          accent-ink  same as ink
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px, doubled on the outer frame
```

## Type

- Display: Chakra Petch 600, capitals, leading 1.0, 32–64px, 0.04em.
- Body: IBM Plex Sans 400 at 16px / 1.6, 52–64ch, white at 84% for long copy.
- Measurements: IBM Plex Mono 400 at 13px for dimensions and tolerances.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- A double keyline frames every major block, 8px apart; nothing is filled.
- Dimension strings draw along the page edges, 1px, with end ticks.
- Photographs are cyanotypes: one blue ink, no colour, printed into the sheet.
- No radius, no shadow — the line is the whole system.

## Range

- An exploded view of the system drawn with 1px lines and dimension strings.
- A specification table in mono, ruled on the outer frame only.
- A statement in Chakra Petch caps held inside a double keyline.

## Motion

None. Hover thickens a line from 1px to 2px in 100ms.

## Voice

Engineering, exact, impersonal. Tolerances, materials, revisions.

## Signature

The dimension string: measured lines with ticks running alongside content, as if the page were a drawing.

## Avoid

Fills, gradients, shadows, colour photography, rounded corners, a second hue, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Console / table · Full-bleed band · Asymmetric split
