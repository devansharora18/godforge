# Theme — Editorial

**Material:** warm white uncoated stock; a printed black with a cool tilt; Bricolage Grotesque condensed at 800 over Source Serif 4; square corners; a heavy ink rule over 1px hairlines; one printer's green.
**Not when:** the page is a tool, a game, or a product demo.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(86% 0.010 95)      paper-2   oklch(91% 0.008 95)
ink        oklch(19% 0.012 265)     muted     oklch(50% 0.010 265)
accent     green 152 · chroma 0.09            accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.006 152)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 6px ink over 1px hairline
```

## Type

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque 800 condensed at 40–88px, leading 0.95, sentence case.
- Body: Source Serif 4 400 at 17px / 1.65, 58–68ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked. Never the display face at small size.
- Display and body differ by family, not by weight.

## Surfaces

- The rule ladder: 6px ink, then a 1px hairline, then the text. Repeated at every section head.
- Square corners; no card, no fill, no shadow — a section is a rule and a column.
- Photographs run full measure in a 5:4 crop, colour, no radius, caption under the hairline.

## Range

- A long read set in two columns with a margin for folios.
- A byline block: name, date, one line of biography, all on hairlines.
- A quote set at display size across both columns, rule above, attribution below.

## Motion

No entrance before the fold. Below it, a 200ms fade per section head, nothing else.

## Voice

Reported, attributed, specific. The head states a fact; the first sentence says who or where.

## Signature

The rule ladder under every section head: 6px, hairline, text — the page's whole hierarchy in three strokes.

## Avoid

Cards, pills, gradients, centred hero, green as decoration, a third family, capitals in the body face.

## Pairs with

Editorial columns · Ledger / index · Colophon close
