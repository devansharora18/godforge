# Theme — Rim

**Material:** warm black paper; warm off-white ink; Switzer 500 with Geist Mono for every measurement; square hairline surfaces; the earth photographed straight down; an orange signal and a sky-blue second.
**Not when:** the page is soft, friendly, or aimed at children.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(18% 0.014 75)      paper-2   oklch(23% 0.016 75)
ink        oklch(95% 0.014 85)      muted     oklch(72% 0.018 80)
accent     orange 45 · chroma 0.15   second  sky 235 · chroma 0.10
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px hairline at 14% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Switzer 500 at 36–72px, leading 1.06, sentence case, −0.01em.
- Body: Switzer 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 54–64ch.
- Measurements: Geist Mono 400 at 13px, tabular, for every dimension and coordinate.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Square hairline surfaces at 1px, 14% ink; nothing filled, nothing rounded.
- Photographs are top-down of terrain, high contrast, printed to the edge of their surface.
- Orange marks an action or a limit; sky blue marks a measurement. Never swapped.
- No shadow; depth comes from the photograph, not from the interface.

## Range

- A coordinate table: point, latitude, elevation — mono, ruled.
- A full-width top-down photograph with two orange marks and a mono label each.
- A three-step sequence where each step is a hairline surface.

## Motion

Minimal: 140ms colour on links, 200ms opacity on photographs. Nothing moves.

## Voice

Field-precise, unsentimental. Coordinates, areas, conditions.

## Signature

The straight-down photograph: the earth seen from above at full width, with mono measurements drawn over it.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, shadows, gradients, a third accent, colour-graded photography, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Console / table · Asymmetric split
