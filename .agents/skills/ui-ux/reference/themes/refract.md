# Theme — Refract

**Material:** sunlit white plaster with real light falling across it; water caustics or prism dispersion rendered live; deep pool-blue ink; Funnel Display at a light weight over Funnel Sans; clear surfaces that cast tinted shadows; one water signal.
**Not when:** the page must feel warm, dark, or expensive.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(97% 0.008 220)     paper-2   oklch(93% 0.014 220)
ink        oklch(35% 0.070 240)     muted     oklch(58% 0.050 235)
accent     water 232 · chroma 0.14              accent-ink  oklch(18% 0.04 232)
edges      radius 16px surfaces · depth tinted shadow · rules 1px at 10% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Funnel Display 300 at 44–96px, leading 1.02, sentence case.
- Body: Funnel Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 54–66ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- The light display weight is the point; no bold anywhere.

## Surfaces

- Caustics move on the ground behind the hero only, never behind running text.
- Surfaces are clear or translucent, with a shadow tinted toward the accent, not grey.
- Radius is 16px on panels, nothing else; 1px hairlines at 10% for divisions.
- Photographs are bright, daylight, high key, no grade.

## Range

- A schedule in a translucent panel with the light moving under it.
- A price beside a single photograph of the water at midday.
- A three-step row where each step is a clear card with a tinted shadow.

## Motion

The page's one live element: caustics drift over 30s, paused under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Cards rise 8px on 240ms.

## Voice

Clear, bright, specific. Say the depth, the hours, the temperature.

## Signature

Live caustics: light moving on the ground under a translucent panel, the only animation on the page.

## Avoid

Dark grounds, glow behind text, grey shadows, heavy weights, sharp corners, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Diptych · Asymmetric split
