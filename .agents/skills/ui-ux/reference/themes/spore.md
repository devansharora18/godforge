# Theme — Spore

**Material:** coffee-ground black with a warm grain; parchment ink; Young Serif over Instrument Sans; soft pills and grained wells; photographs lit by lamps and candles; one oyster pink.
**Not when:** the page must feel clinical, bright, or fast.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(19% 0.018 60)      paper-2   oklch(24% 0.022 60)
ink        oklch(92% 0.024 85)      muted     oklch(70% 0.026 75)
accent     oyster 30 · chroma 0.09             accent-ink  oklch(19% 0.03 30)
edges      radius 999px pills, 18px wells · depth none · rules none
```

## Type

- Display: Young Serif 400 at 38–76px, leading 1.06, sentence case.
- Body: Instrument Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.65, 52–62ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- The serif never appears below 30px.

## Surfaces

- Wells are the paper a step lighter with an 18px radius and 4% grain inside them.
- Pills are the only fully round element; nothing else is above 18px.
- Photographs are warm and low, lit by one lamp, no frame, no radius change.
- No rules and no shadows; the grain does the separating.

## Range

- A three-step guide, each step a grained well with a number in the serif.
- A testimonial in a warm well with the name in the body face.
- A price in Young Serif at 64px with the oyster mark beside it.

## Motion

Gentle: 260ms opacity and a 4px rise. Nothing else moves.

## Voice

Warm, plain, practical. Say the dose, the time, the effect.

## Signature

The grained well: every panel is the same warm black with visible grain, one step lighter, so the page feels lit rather than drawn.

## Avoid

Glow, neon, white surfaces, sharp corners, cool greys, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Editorial columns · Colophon close
