# Theme — Dusk

**Material:** a real landscape at the turn of the light behind the hero; slate below it; the product in smoked glass with a lit top edge; Wix Madefor Display at a quiet weight over its text cut; bone wells for light surfaces; one periwinkle signal and one apricot.
**Not when:** the page must be printable, or the product is a document.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(26% 0.020 268)     paper-2   oklch(31% 0.022 268)
ink        oklch(95% 0.008 250)     muted     oklch(74% 0.014 260)
accent     periwinkle 274 · chroma 0.12    second  apricot 55 · chroma 0.10
edges      radius 12px · depth 1px top light · rules 1px at 12% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Wix Madefor Display 500 at 38–76px, leading 1.06, sentence case.
- Body: Wix Madefor Text 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 52–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- No bold; the quiet weight against the photograph is the hierarchy.

## Surfaces

- The hero's photograph is a real landscape; below it the page is slate to the footer.
- Smoked glass panels: 12% lighter than the ground, 12px radius, 1px lighter top edge.
- Bone wells carry the light content — the only near-white surfaces on the page.
- No glow behind text; light comes from the photograph and the top edges.

## Range

- A three-step row on bone wells, dark text, inside the slate page.
- A single product surface in smoked glass, lit from above.
- A price on bone, the periwinkle used once as the figure's underline.

## Motion

Slow: 400ms opacity on the hero image, 200ms on panels. Nothing scales.

## Voice

Calm, assured, specific. Say what the product does at the end of a day.

## Signature

The real landscape: one photograph of an actual place at dusk fills the hero, and the interface below is built to sit under it.

## Avoid

Gradient skies, glow, neon, white cards, a third accent, synthetic illustrations, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Asymmetric split · Diptych
