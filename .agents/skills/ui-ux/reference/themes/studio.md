# Theme — Studio

**Material:** cool studio-white proof paper with an ink dot grid and grain; blue-black ink; Hedvig Letters Serif heads over Geist; crop marks on printed pieces and orange construction guides; the work photographed in true colour; one cadmium orange.
**Not when:** the page must look finished, corporate, or reassuring.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(96% 0.005 215)     paper-2   oklch(92% 0.006 215)
ink        oklch(22% 0.025 265)     muted     oklch(52% 0.015 265)
accent     cadmium 52 · chroma 0.19            accent-ink  oklch(18% 0.03 52)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px ink plus 8px crop marks
```

## Type

- Display: Hedvig Letters Serif 400 at 40–84px, leading 1.03, sentence case.
- Body: Geist 400 at 16px / 1.6, 50–62ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- The serif carries heads and captions; the grotesk carries everything else.

## Surfaces

- An ink dot grid at 24px pitch across the ground at 6% opacity.
- Every image and column carries 8px crop marks at the corners in ink.
- Orange appears only as a construction guide: a 1px rule, an arrow, a measurement bracket.
- Photographs are true colour, flat, no shadow, sitting directly on the proof.

## Range

- A work index: title, year, medium, ruled in three columns.
- A single image with crop marks and a 1px orange measure across its width.
- A caption block in the serif at 24px beside the image, not under it.

## Motion

None on entry. Hover draws a 1px orange rule under the label in 120ms.

## Voice

Dry, credited, specific. Title, year, medium, client — no superlatives.

## Signature

The crop marks: every piece on the page is marked like artwork on a proof, which makes the whole page read as a studio sheet.

## Avoid

Shadows, rounded corners, gradients, centred hero, colour photography of anything but the work, orange as decoration.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Editorial columns · Colophon close
