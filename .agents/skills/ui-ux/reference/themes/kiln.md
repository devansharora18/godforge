# Theme — Kiln

**Material:** near-black ground; cream ink; Boska over Geist; smoked wells; photographs lit only by the work; a heat ramp that marks temperature; one ember signal.
**Not when:** the page must look clean, digital, or cold.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(16% 0.012 40)      paper-2   oklch(21% 0.016 40)
ink        oklch(94% 0.018 80)      muted     oklch(70% 0.020 60)
accent     ember 45 · chroma 0.16              accent-ink  oklch(18% 0.03 45)
ramp       900–1200 °C · oklch(50% 0.12 40) → oklch(80% 0.16 75)
edges      radius 8px wells · depth none · rules none
```

## Type

- Display: Boska 500 at 44–88px, leading 1.02, sentence case.
- Body: Geist 400 at 16px / 1.6, 52–64ch, cream never pure white.
- Figures: Geist 500 tabular, paired with the ramp where a temperature is the content.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Wells are smoked: the paper a step lighter with an 8px radius, no border, no shadow.
- The heat ramp is the only gradient on the page, and it only ever marks temperature.
- Photographs are lit by the work itself — the object is the light source.
- No rules; separation by lightness.

## Range

- A firing schedule: temperature on the ramp, time in tabular figures.
- A glaze or clay sample set in four smoked wells, named in the body face.
- A single piece photographed in the dark with only its own light.

## Motion

Slow and warm: 400ms opacity on wells. Nothing slides.

## Voice

Material, exact, unhurried. Say the clay, the cone, the temperature.

## Signature

The heat ramp: a real colour scale that appears only where temperature matters, the one place colour is allowed to run.

## Avoid

Glow behind text, neon, white surfaces, sharp corners, a second signal, colour photographs outside the ramp.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Diptych · Editorial columns
