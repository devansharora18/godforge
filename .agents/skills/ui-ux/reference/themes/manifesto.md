# Theme — Manifesto

**Material:** warm near-black ground; warm off-white ink; Anton capitals at poster size, leaded tight, over a quiet Public Sans body; hairlines, a heavy ink bar, square edges, one fighting red.
**Not when:** the page must reassure, or the reader skims on a phone.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(19% 0.010 60)      paper-2   oklch(24% 0.012 60)
ink        oklch(93% 0.012 80)      muted     oklch(72% 0.014 70)
accent     red 28 · chroma 0.17                accent-ink  oklch(96% 0.010 28)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules hairline at 16% ink, one heavy bar
```

## Type

- Display: Anton 400, capitals, leading 0.95, 56–148px, 0.01em. It occupies most of a screen.
- Body: Public Sans 400 at 16.5px / 1.6, 50–60ch, never more than four lines together.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- No weight between 400 and the display.

## Surfaces

- The heavy ink bar: one 12px off-white rule that opens the manifesto, once per page.
- Everything else is a hairline at 16% ink; nothing is filled.
- Photographs are near-black and white, dropped to 60% opacity so the ground shows through.
- No shadows, no radius, no glow on the dark.

## Range

- A numbered article set at 72px, one clause per screen.
- A signature block: one name, one date, in the body face.
- A red line of text set against the dark, once, as the only celebration.

## Motion

None. The page is read in stills; a 120ms colour change on links is the whole budget.

## Voice

First person, plural, absolute. Short clauses, no hedges, no marketing verbs.

## Signature

The heavy ink bar and the poster capitals beneath it — the page opens like a printed declaration, not a landing page.

## Avoid

Gradients, glows, rounded corners, soft shadows, a second accent, italic emphasis, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Oversized type · Poster stack · Full-bleed band
