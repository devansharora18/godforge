# Theme — Garden

**Material:** warm oat paper; deep green ink; Vollkorn 600 with old-style figures over Hanken Grotesk; hairlines and soft moss or clay tints; daylight photographs in true colour; one leaf-green signal.
**Not when:** the page is dark-mode native, or the product is a dashboard.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(90% 0.018 90)      paper-2   oklch(94% 0.014 90)
ink        oklch(28% 0.040 150)     muted     oklch(54% 0.030 150)
accent     leaf 140 · chroma 0.10             accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.008 140)
edges      radius 3px · depth none · rules 1px hairline at 20% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Vollkorn 600 at 34–64px, leading 1.05, sentence case, old-style figures on.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 400 at 16.5px / 1.65, 55–66ch.
- Figures: Vollkorn 600, old-style, at 40–64px when the number is the content.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Moss and clay tints at 8% for alternating bands; never a white card.
- Hairlines at 20% ink; no heavy rules, no shadows.
- Photographs in true daylight colour, full measure, 3px radius, no caption box.
- Photograph treatment: natural, no filter.

## Range

- A season table: month, what is ready, what to do — ruled, in the serif.
- A price or a count set enormous in old-style figures.
- A note in the margin beside a tall photograph, body copy at 40ch.

## Motion

Gentle: 300ms opacity and a 6px rise per section. The page never bounces or scales.

## Voice

Practical, seasonal, unhurried. Say the month, the variety, the quantity.

## Signature

Old-style figures at display size: a number that sits on the baseline with its descenders showing, carrying a whole section.

## Avoid

Dark grounds, gradients, mono labels, saturated greens, more than one tint per band, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Editorial columns · Asymmetric split · Diptych
