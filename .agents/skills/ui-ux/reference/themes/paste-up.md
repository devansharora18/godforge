# Theme — Paste-up

**Material:** kraft board with a fibre wash; near-black toner; League Gothic capitals over a Newsreader galley; white copy-paper lifts; hard ink offsets; one sheet of fluorescent yellow.
**Not when:** the page must look settled, or the product is enterprise software.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(72% 0.045 70)      paper-2   oklch(96% 0.004 80)
ink        oklch(24% 0.012 60)      muted     oklch(50% 0.014 60)
accent     fluorescent 112 · chroma 0.20 · L 88   accent-ink  oklch(20% 0.02 112)
edges      radius 0 · depth hard 3px offset · rules 1px toner
```

## Type

- Display: League Gothic 400, capitals, leading 0.9, 48–120px, 0.01em.
- Body: Newsreader 400 at 16.5px / 1.55, 48–58ch, set as a galley column with 1px rules.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Never set League Gothic below 32px.

## Surfaces

- White copy-paper panels sit on the kraft, rotated up to 0.6°, each with a hard 3px ink offset.
- Fibre wash across the board at 10%, irregular at the edges.
- One fluorescent sheet per page — a note, a price, a warning.
- Photographs are photocopied: one ink, high contrast, cut square.

## Range

- A paste-up of three notes at slight angles, each holding one fact.
- A price on the fluorescent sheet, rotated, with a toner rule under it.
- A galley column of body text with a 1px rule and a folio at the foot.

## Motion

None. Hover lifts a sheet by 1px and settles it in 100ms.

## Voice

Urgent, practical, handwritten in spirit. Imperatives and dates.

## Signature

The rotated copy-paper lift: content sits on white sheets stuck onto the kraft at an angle, each casting solid toner.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, gradients, soft shadows, full-colour photography, straightened composition everywhere, a second fluorescent sheet.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Editorial columns · Horizontal band sequence
