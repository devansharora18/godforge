# Theme — Halftone

**Material:** butter-cream stock; brown-black ink; Ultra heads over Chivo; 3px rules and square wells; one vermilion printed solid and through a dot screen; photographs in full colour.
**Not when:** the page is corporate, financial, or has to look quiet.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(94% 0.045 90)      paper-2   oklch(89% 0.055 90)
ink        oklch(24% 0.030 45)      muted     oklch(52% 0.032 45)
accent     vermilion 33 · chroma 0.18          accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.010 33)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 3px ink, square wells
```

## Type

- Display: Ultra 400 at 44–104px, leading 0.94, sentence case. Heavy, slab, unmissable.
- Body: Chivo 400 at 17px / 1.55, 52–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- One weight per role; no italic.

## Surfaces

- The dot screen: a 6px halftone at 45° fills accent blocks and image edges, never text.
- Wells are square, ruled at 3px, with 20px of padding — never rounded.
- Photographs print in full colour inside a screen-edged frame.
- Accent blocks are solid vermilion; the halftone is the second voice of the same ink.

## Range

- A three-step row where each step is a ruled well with a number in Ultra.
- A price in Ultra at 72px, halftone behind it, copy beneath.
- A photo grid of two up, each with a 3px rule and a caption in Chivo.

## Motion

Press-like: 120ms on transform of 2px, no easing overshoot, applied to controls only.

## Voice

Warm, funny, specific. Say the flavour, the size, the hour.

## Signature

The dot screen: one accent ink printed twice — solid for type, screened for fills — so the page looks like a two-pass job on cheap stock.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, soft shadows, gradients, a second accent, thin display weights, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Diptych · Horizontal band sequence
