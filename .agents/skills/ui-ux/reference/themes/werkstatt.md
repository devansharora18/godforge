# Theme — Werkstatt

**Material:** warm putty ground with a lighter well; near-black ink; Jost 700 capitals and numerals over Karla; square 2px and 3px ink rules boxing cells; flat ultramarine, vermilion and yellow primaries.
**Not when:** the page must look organic, or the subject is luxury.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(82% 0.020 82)      paper-2   oklch(88% 0.018 82)
ink        oklch(20% 0.014 60)      muted     oklch(48% 0.016 60)
accent     ultramarine 264 · chroma 0.19   second vermilion 32 · chroma 0.18   third yellow 95 · chroma 0.17
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 2px and 3px ink, boxed cells
```

## Type

- Display: Jost 700, capitals, leading 0.98, 36–76px, 0.01em.
- Body: Karla 400 at 16.5px / 1.55, 52–64ch.
- Numerals: Jost 700, tabular, boxed in their own cell.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- Every block is boxed by 2px ink; the page's outer measure is 3px.
- Primaries fill whole cells — one per cell, never two in one box.
- A lighter well in the putty holds tables; the ground is never white.
- Photographs sit inside a ruled cell, full colour, cropped square.

## Range

- A specification table where every cell is a box and figures are in Jost.
- A parts list: number, name, quantity — ruled, one primary per row.
- A statement in caps across three cells, one cell yellow.

## Motion

Mechanical: 120ms on background change for interactive cells. No movement of any element.

## Voice

Technical, precise, workshop-plain. Part numbers and measurements.

## Signature

The boxed cell: the whole page is a ruled table of cells, and colour appears only as a filled cell.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, shadows, gradients, tints of the primaries, a fourth colour, centred hero, tracked labels.

## Pairs with

Console / table · Horizontal band sequence · Diptych
