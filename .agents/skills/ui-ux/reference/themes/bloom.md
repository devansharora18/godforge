# Theme — Bloom

**Material:** warm off-white paper with grain and a faint working grid; warm umber ink; Ranade heads with one roman figure; two quiet terracotta forms on the page; parts lit from above on rounded clips.
**Not when:** the page must feel fast, cheap, or technical.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(93% 0.020 72)      paper-2   oklch(88% 0.028 68)
ink        oklch(30% 0.035 60)      muted     oklch(58% 0.028 62)
accent     terracotta 36 · chroma 0.10         accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.010 36)
edges      radius 10px · depth none · rules 1px at 12% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Ranade 600 at 42–84px, leading 1.04, sentence case.
- Body: Ranade 400 at 16.5px / 1.68, 54–66ch.
- One figure in a roman serif (Gambarino) at 28px, used once, for the statement that carries the page.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- A 24px working grid at 4% and grain at 6% over the paper.
- Two terracotta forms, flat, one behind the hero and one closing the page. Never three.
- Photographs are lit from above on a rounded clip, with a soft even shadow under the object.
- Hairlines at 12%; nothing is boxed.

## Range

- A set of three parts laid out as if on a bench, each labelled in body size.
- A statement in the roman serif between two terracotta rules.
- A close photograph of a mechanism with 40px margin, no frame.

## Motion

Slow and light: 420ms opacity with a 2% scale on images. Text never moves.

## Voice

Careful, tactile, specific. Name the part, the material, the tolerance.

## Signature

The two terracotta forms: the only saturated thing on the page, anchoring the hero and the close, with nothing between them.

## Avoid

Saturated gradients, dark grounds, more than two terracotta forms, sharp corners, stock lifestyle photos, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Diptych · Colophon close
