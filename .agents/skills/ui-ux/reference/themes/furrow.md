# Theme — Furrow

**Material:** warm manila paper; near-black ink in rules; a generated survey map in sienna contours and slate water; Red Hat Display 600 over Red Hat Text with Red Hat Mono figures; moulded 6px keys; a waterproof notebook card; one tangerine signal and a grass green for what is in date.
**Not when:** the page is a city product, a gallery, or a dark-mode app.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(85% 0.030 85)      paper-2   oklch(89% 0.026 85)
ink        oklch(24% 0.012 60)      muted     oklch(50% 0.020 60)
accent     tangerine 40 · chroma 0.16    in-date  grass 140 · chroma 0.10
edges      radius 6px keys · depth 1px press · rules 1px at 18% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Red Hat Display 600 at 34–64px, leading 1.06, sentence case.
- Body: Red Hat Text 400 at 16.5px / 1.65, 52–64ch.
- Figures: Red Hat Mono 400 at 14px, tabular, for area, date and count.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.

## Surfaces

- The survey map is generated from real values: sienna contours, slate water, 1px lines.
- Keys are moulded: 6px radius, 1px press, no shadow, 44px tall.
- Photographs are top-down of ground, field or paper, no frame.
- Rules at 18% ink mark table rows; nothing is boxed.

## Range

- A field record: block, crop, date — mono figures, ruled rows.
- A map at full width with three labelled points and a legend in body size.
- A list where each row ends in the green or tangerine state mark.

## Motion

Practical: 160ms on key press, 200ms opacity on rows. Nothing decorative.

## Voice

Plain, agricultural, exact. Say the block, the acreage, the date.

## Signature

The survey map: contours and water drawn from the page's own data, the one graphic the material allows.

## Avoid

Rounded cards, gradients, soft shadows, saturated photography, a third accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Console / table · Asymmetric split · Full-bleed band
