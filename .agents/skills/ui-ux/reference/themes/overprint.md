# Theme — Overprint

**Material:** grey newsprint stock; near-black ink; wide heavy Anybody capitals over Archivo; flat square fills; a teal pass and an orange pass that print moss where they cross; photographs pulled through one pass.
**Not when:** the page needs precision, or the reader must trust a number at a glance.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(90% 0.008 250)     paper-2   oklch(85% 0.010 250)
ink        oklch(20% 0.008 250)     muted     oklch(48% 0.010 250)
accent     teal 212 · chroma 0.12   second  orange 40 · chroma 0.18   cross  oklch(48% 0.09 140)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px, fills carry the weight
```

## Type

- Display: Anybody 800 expanded at 40–100px, capitals, leading 0.95, 0.02em.
- Body: Archivo 400 at 16.5px / 1.55, 52–66ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Two weights only: 400 body, 800 display.

## Surfaces

- Two inks over one another: teal blocks, orange blocks, moss where they overlap. Never a tint of either.
- Fills are flat and square; no stroke, no radius, no shadow.
- Photographs print through one pass, so they read teal or orange, never full colour.
- Paper grain at 6%; the stock is visibly grey.

## Range

- A two-column comparison where each column is an ink pass.
- A schedule where the overlap of two passes marks a double booking.
- A wordmark-scale statement in heavy caps across the full measure.

## Motion

None. Hover swaps a block from one ink to the other in 120ms.

## Voice

Opinionated, plain, in capital letters when it matters. No softening.

## Signature

The moss overlap: two flat inks crossing to make a third colour that exists only where they print.

## Avoid

Tints, gradients, rounded corners, shadows, colour photographs, a third ink, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Full-bleed band · Horizontal band sequence
