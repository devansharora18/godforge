# Theme — Ribbon

**Material:** warm off-white by day or blue-black at night; a quiet Instrument Sans over Figtree; machined parts lit from above; the hour drawn as light on a canvas; one teal signal.
**Not when:** the page is a shop, a form, or a document.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(94% 0.010 85)      night  oklch(18% 0.014 250)
ink        oklch(24% 0.012 70)      muted  oklch(54% 0.012 250)
accent     teal 190 · chroma 0.12              accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.008 190)
edges      radius 999px controls, 20px panels · depth 1px top light
```

## Type

- Display: Instrument Sans 400 at 40–72px, leading 1.06, sentence case, a quiet weight.
- Body: Figtree 400 at 16.5px / 1.62, 52–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- No bold display; a heavier body is the only contrast.

## Surfaces

- Day and night are two complete token sets, swapped as a whole, never mixed.
- Panels are 20px radius with a 1px lighter top edge, no shadow.
- Photographs are machined objects lit from above on the page's own paper, no frame.
- The canvas element draws the hour as light — the page's one graphic.

## Range

- A sleep or session table: time, duration, quality — ruled lightly.
- A single object photographed top-lit with one line of caption beneath.
- A schedule as a horizontal ribbon of time blocks.

## Motion

Soft and slow: 300ms fades; the canvas eases one value over 2s. Nothing bounces.

## Voice

Calm, low, precise. Say the hour, the duration, the change.

## Signature

The hour as light: a generated canvas gradient whose colour and height track the actual time, the only colour on the page.

## Avoid

Bright accents, hard shadows, sharp corners, more than one panel shape, stock sleep imagery, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Full-bleed band · Editorial columns
