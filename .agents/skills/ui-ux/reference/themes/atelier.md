# Theme — Atelier

**Material:** limewash paper under a slow luminous air; deep umber ink; Sentient light over Hanken Grotesk; cotton card stock with visible fibre, hairlines, prints matted in warm daylight, one cool slate signal.
**Not when:** the page has to read fast, or the subject is software.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(94.5% 0.012 76)    paper-2   oklch(97% 0.008 76)
ink        oklch(26% 0.025 52)      muted     oklch(56% 0.018 60)
accent     slate 248 · chroma 0.06             accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.004 248)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px hairline, one long rule under the fold
```

## Type

- Display: Sentient 300 at 56–120px, leading 0.98, sentence case. Italic is a second voice, never an accent word.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 400 at 16px / 1.7, 55–62ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Two sizes above 32px on a page, not four.

## Surfaces

- Limewash ground with a 3% fibre texture at 7% opacity.
- Photographs matted, not bled: 40px of paper on all four sides, warm light, no border.
- Hairlines at 14% ink; one rule heavier where a section ends.
- Photograph treatment: warm, `con=-6 vib=-10`.

## Range

- A single object photographed on the matte with its caption in the margin.
- A price set in the display face, alone on a half page.
- A material swatch row: four paper squares, each named in the body face.

## Motion

Slow: 600ms opacity with a 2% scale, once per section. Nothing loops, nothing parallaxes.

## Voice

Quiet and specific. Name the material — limewash, oak, unglazed — never the feeling it is meant to give.

## Signature

Pressed type: the display weighted into the stock with a 0.5px ink edge and the fibre showing through the counters.

## Avoid

Rounded corners, gradients, neon, stock interiors, centred hero, a third type family, more than one slate mark.

## Pairs with

Oversized type · Asymmetric split · Full-bleed band
