# Theme — Darkroom

**Material:** warm print-black ground with a fine light grain; fibre-white ink; hairline Sofia Sans Extra Condensed capitals over Barlow; zero radius; photographs printed deeper than the ground; one amber safelight.
**Not when:** the page needs colour, or the reader is on a phone in daylight.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(17% 0.008 55)      paper-2   oklch(21% 0.010 55)
ink        oklch(94% 0.010 80)      muted     oklch(70% 0.014 70)
accent     amber 58 · chroma 0.13              accent-ink  oklch(19% 0.03 58)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px hairline at 14% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 600, capitals, leading 1.0, 34–72px, 0.02em.
- Body: Barlow 400 at 16.5px / 1.6, 52–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Head lengths stay under five words; the face is too narrow for more.

## Surfaces

- Grain at 8% over the black; a light vignette only at the page edges, never behind text.
- Photographs print darker than the ground, so they read as windows rather than panels.
- Hairlines only; no fills, no cards, no radius.
- The amber appears as one small mark — a safelight, never a wash.

## Range

- A contact sheet: six frames in a grid, each with a caption in body size.
- A single print at full width with its exposure data in the margin.
- A series list: number, title, year — hairline rows.

## Motion

Nothing. Images fade in at 300ms on load; that is the entire motion.

## Voice

Sparse, technical, personal. Exposure, date, place — no adjectives.

## Signature

Prints darker than the page: the images sit below the black, so the screen reads as a darkroom rather than a gallery.

## Avoid

Glow, bloom, gradients, coloured photographs, rounded corners, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Full-bleed band · Asymmetric split · Editorial columns
