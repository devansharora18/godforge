# Theme — Ultraviolet

**Material:** lavender paper by day or violet-black at night; Outfit set lowercase over Onest; hairline pills; one saturated violet in two strengths; photographs pulled into the violet.
**Not when:** the page must feel handmade, or the product is a physical object.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(94% 0.030 300)     night  oklch(19% 0.060 290)
ink        oklch(24% 0.040 295)     muted  oklch(54% 0.040 295)
accent     violet 290 · chroma 0.19 · two strengths: L 55 and L 75
edges      radius 999px pills, 10px panels · depth none · rules 1px at 12% ink
```

## Type

- Display: Outfit 600 at 40–80px, leading 1.04, lowercase, −0.02em.
- Body: Onest 400 at 16.5px / 1.6, 54–64ch.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- The lowercase display is the voice; never set it in capitals.

## Surfaces

- Everything is a pill or a 10px panel; nothing is a right angle.
- Two strengths of one violet: the deep one for text and marks, the light one for fields.
- Photographs are pulled into the violet by a duotone, one per screen.
- Day and night swap as complete token sets; never mix them.

## Range

- A three-step row of pills, each holding one word and a number.
- A statement set lowercase at 72px with the light violet field behind it.
- A duotone photograph at full width with the deep violet as its shadow.

## Motion

Fluid: 260ms on panels, 180ms on pill hover. Nothing bounces.

## Voice

Modern, direct, lowercase in spirit. Say the action, the count, the price.

## Signature

One hue in two strengths: the same violet carries both the type and the fields, so the page has no second colour idea.

## Avoid

Uppercase display, a second hue, sharp corners, shadows, warm photography, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Diptych · Horizontal band sequence · Asymmetric split
