# Theme — Solstice

**Material:** cream paper; warm brown ink; Yeseva One over Archivo and Archivo Narrow; gold hairlines and hard paper shadows; photographs in low warm light; one vermilion.
**Not when:** the page must read cool, technical, or fast.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(93% 0.022 85)      paper-2   oklch(88% 0.028 85)
ink        oklch(30% 0.045 55)      muted     oklch(56% 0.030 60)
accent     vermilion 24 · chroma 0.16          accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.010 24)
gold       oklch(72% 0.070 80)      edges radius 0 · depth 2px hard offset
```

## Type

- Display: Yeseva One 400 at 40–76px, leading 1.04, sentence case, generous sidebearings.
- Body: Archivo 400 at 16.5px / 1.64, 54–64ch. Figures in Archivo Narrow 500 when tabular.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked.
- Never set Yeseva One under 30px.

## Surfaces

- Gold hairlines at 1px mark every division; one heavier rule under the hero.
- Panels cast 2px of solid brown ink offset down-right, no blur, like cut paper.
- Photographs in low warm light, matted with 3px radius and a 2px ink edge.
- Cream is never pure white; the accent never fills a block larger than a bar.

## Range

- A date and address set in the display face as the closing section.
- A menu or programme: name in the serif, note in narrow grotesk, price right.
- A photograph at half width with a caption in the margin, gold rule above.

## Motion

Warm and slow: 380ms opacity and a 4px rise per section head.

## Voice

Inviting, concrete, dated. Say the place, the hour, the address — no atmosphere words.

## Signature

The hard paper shadow: every panel is a cut sheet sitting 2px above the cream, never a soft drop shadow.

## Avoid

Cool greys, pure white, blurred shadows, rounded corners, a second accent, gradients, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Asymmetric split · Diptych · Colophon close
