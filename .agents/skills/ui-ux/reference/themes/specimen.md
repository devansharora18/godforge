# Theme — Specimen

**Material:** warm oat paper; warm near-black ink; razor-hairline Bodoni Moda over Geist; square 1px rules; one ink-blue signal that also draws the metric guides.
**Not when:** the page is a product page with a lot to say, or the reader needs urgency.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(91% 0.014 80)      paper-2   oklch(87% 0.016 80)
ink        oklch(23% 0.010 60)      muted     oklch(50% 0.012 60)
accent     ink-blue 262 · chroma 0.10          accent-ink  oklch(97% 0.006 262)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 1px, drawn as guides
```

## Type

- Display: Bodoni Moda 400 at 52–120px, leading 1.0, sentence case, hairline weight only.
- Body: Geist 400 at 16px / 1.6, 50–60ch, small against the display.
- Figures: Geist tabular for specimens, describing size and weight.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked, sitting on the guide.

## Surfaces

- The metric guide: a 1px blue baseline, x-height and cap-height line drawn behind a specimen word.
- Everything else is a 1px ink rule; no fills, no shadows.
- Photographs appear once, as a grey duotone, inside a guide.
- Paper is warm; the blue is the only cool thing.

## Range

- A specimen block: one letter at 120px with three blue guides and its measurements in tabular figures.
- A weight ladder: the same word at four weights, ruled between.
- A price set in Bodoni with the guide running under the figures.

## Motion

None. Typography does not animate; a 120ms colour change on links is the limit.

## Voice

Precise and technical about the thing itself: weight, size, spacing, date cut.

## Signature

The blue metric guide: a hairline baseline and x-height crossing a giant letter, the only colour on the page.

## Avoid

Fills, shadows, gradients, rounded corners, bold body text, a second accent, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Oversized type · Ledger / index · Editorial columns
