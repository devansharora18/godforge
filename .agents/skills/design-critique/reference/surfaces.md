# Surfaces

Open the checklist that matches the screen. A landing page and a settings table fail in different ways.

## Landing page

- The offer is legible in five seconds: what it is, who it is for, what to do next.
- One primary action. Secondary actions are visually quieter.
- Section rhythm varies; no two adjacent sections share a layout.
- Every claim has evidence: a screenshot, a number, a name, a demo.
- No invented logos, testimonials, or metrics.
- The page survives a 360px fold without an unreadable headline.
- Footer carries the real links, not a second CTA card.

## Pricing

- Plans are comparable at a glance: same row order, aligned features, one recommended plan.
- The recommended plan is marked by position and weight, not only color.
- Billing period and currency are explicit; totals update visibly.
- Feature differences read as sentences, not checkmark soup.
- "Contact us" has a next step, not a dead link.
- Fine print is readable, not 10px grey.

## Dashboard

- The top of the page answers "is anything wrong?" before showing charts.
- Primary metric has context: comparison, period, and unit.
- Empty state explains how data arrives; zero is not an error.
- Tables have sticky headers, sortable columns, and a visible row count.
- Filters show what is active and have a one-click reset.
- Dense is fine; cramped is not. 32–40px rows, 12–14px type.

## Form / checkout

- One column. Labels above inputs, persistent — never placeholder-only.
- Required vs optional is stated once, consistently.
- Errors appear next to the field, say what to do, and are announced to screen readers.
- The primary action states the outcome ("Pay $48", not "Submit").
- Progress is visible for multi-step; back does not lose data.
- No dark patterns: no pre-checked upsells, no hidden fees revealed at the end.

## Auth

- One job per screen; sign-in and sign-up are not tabs if the flow differs.
- Password rules appear before typing, not after failing.
- Errors do not reveal whether an account exists.
- Social login buttons are real, labeled, and ordered consistently.
- Recovery path is visible without scrolling.

## Empty / error / loading

- Empty explains the value and the first action, with a real example.
- Error says what happened, what was preserved, and what to do next.
- Loading is shaped like the content (skeleton), not a centered spinner.
- Retry is present and does not lose the user's input.

## Settings

- Grouped by task, not by database table.
- Destructive actions are separated, named, and confirmed.
- Save is explicit, or autosave is unmistakably indicated.
- Current values are visible without opening each row.
