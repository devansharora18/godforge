# Theme — Riso

**Material:** bright uncoated stock with no black; a medium blue drum for every word, a fluorescent pink drum for the fills, purple where they cross; Cabinet Grotesk 800 over Literata; grain; the head printing a hair out of register.
**Not when:** the page needs to look expensive, corporate, or accessible at any size.

## Tokens

```
paper      oklch(94% 0.012 95)      paper-2   oklch(90% 0.020 95)
ink        blue 255 · chroma 0.16 · L 34       (never black)
fill       pink 356 · chroma 0.19 · L 62       cross  oklch(45% 0.12 310)
edges      radius 0 · depth none · rules 2px blue, misregistered by 1px
```

## Type

- Display: Cabinet Grotesk 800 at 40–96px, leading 0.98, sentence case.
- Body: Literata 400 at 17px / 1.6, 54–64ch. The serif is the counter-voice to the heavy sans.
- Labels: body face a step smaller, sentence case, untracked, printed blue.
- Two colours only: blue ink and pink fill. Purple happens where they overlap, never as a chosen third.

## Surfaces

- Every rule and border is blue at 2px, offset 1px from its box like a bad registration.
- Fills are fluorescent pink at full strength, never tinted.
- Photographs print in two passes: blue, then pink, meeting in purple.
- Grain at 7%; the stock is visibly paper.

## Range

- A price set in the heavy sans on a pink field, blue type inside it.
- A two-colour photograph at full width, cropped hard.
- A list where each row's number sits in a pink block.

## Motion

None. Hover shifts a border by 1px and back in 100ms, like a sheet settling.

## Voice

Direct, local, a bit loud. Names and dates, no hedging.

## Signature

Misregistration: every heavy element sits 1px off its outline, so the page reads as printed in two passes on one machine.

## Avoid

Black, gradients, more than two inks, rounded corners, shadows, a third colour chosen on purpose, centred hero.

## Pairs with

Poster stack · Horizontal band sequence · Diptych
